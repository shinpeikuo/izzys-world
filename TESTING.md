# Testing Izzy's World on the tablet

Do this on Izzy's tablet with the game installed from
https://shinpeikuo.github.io/izzys-world/ (Chrome menu ⋮ → **Add to Home screen** / **Install app**).
Tick each box. Anything that fails: write down what you did and what happened, and tell Claude.

## 1. Install and update
- [ ] Open the game from its home-screen icon. It fills the whole screen, with no address bar.
- [ ] Hold the gear (top left) for 2 seconds. The grown-up area shows the newest version number.
  If it shows an older one: close the game fully (swipe it away in the recent-apps view), reopen it,
  wait 10 seconds, and repeat once more.
- [ ] The grown-up area says **Offline: ready to play offline** and **Storage: kept safe by Chrome**.

## 2. Offline
- [ ] Turn on **Airplane mode**. Close the game fully and open it again.
- [ ] It loads, and you can visit the house, café and castle, with all pictures showing.
- [ ] Sounds and music still play.
- [ ] Turn Airplane mode off again.

## 3. The world is kept
- [ ] Move some furniture, put a few people and pets in different rooms, sit someone down,
  give someone something to hold, switch a lamp on.
- [ ] Close the game fully and reopen it. Everything is exactly where you left it.
- [ ] Restart the tablet and open the game. Still all there.
- [ ] Grown-up area → **Save backup**. A file appears in Downloads.
- [ ] Change something in the game, then **Load backup** with that file → **Replace**.
  The world goes back to how it was when you saved the backup.

## 4. Update without losing the world
Do this the next time Claude publishes an update (or ask Claude for a test update):
- [ ] Before updating, take note of a few things in the world (who is where).
- [ ] Open the game online, close it fully, reopen it. The version number is the new one.
- [ ] Everything you noted is still there.

## 5. Touch (best done by Izzy, while you watch)
- [ ] She can drag furniture, people and pets with one finger without them "slipping".
- [ ] Small things (cups, crowns, cakes) are easy to pick up.
- [ ] The arrows, map, box, people, music and sound buttons all work on the first tap.
- [ ] Two fingers can drag two things at once.
- [ ] Dropping a person on a chair, sofa or bed sits or lays them down.
- [ ] Dropping a small item on a person's hand makes them hold it.
- [ ] Tapping Izzy opens her outfits; tapping one changes her outfit.
- [ ] Pressing the tablet's **back** button once does not close the game.
- [ ] Turning the tablet upright shows the "turn me" screen; turning it back continues the game.

## 6. Speed and sound
- [ ] The game starts within a few seconds (the yellow loading bar fills).
- [ ] Dragging is smooth, even in a room full of things.
- [ ] Moving between rooms is quick.
- [ ] Sounds: tapping, picking up, dropping, throwing away, the dog, cat and peacock.
- [ ] Each place (map, house, café, castle) has its own tune.
- [ ] The music and sound buttons switch them off and on, and stay that way after reopening.
- [ ] Music stops when you leave the game (home button) and comes back when you return.
